WHAT HAPPENS IF YOU DO NOTHING

Three things happen the moment your family has to go to probate court.

None of them are things most families expect — and all three are avoidable.

01

It becomes public record

What you owned, what you owed, and who inherits it becomes part of a searchable court file — open to anyone who looks, including people who look for a living.

02

Scammers get there first

Probate filings are actively monitored. Families often hear from someone claiming a fee is owed before assets are released — timed to arrive while you're still grieving.

03

It's slow, and it costs upfront

A straightforward case commonly takes nine months to two years. Filing fees, bonds, and attorney costs are often paid out of pocket before the estate can reimburse anyone.
